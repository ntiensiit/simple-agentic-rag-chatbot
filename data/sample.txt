ORBITAL TEA HOUSE KNOWLEDGE BASE

The Orbital Tea House is a fictional cafe located on Lantern Dock. It opened in 2042 and serves tea, soup, and astronomy workshops.

The house signature drink is Comet Oolong. It is made with roasted oolong tea, pear syrup, and a small pinch of sea salt. It is served warm, never iced.

Opening hours are Tuesday through Sunday, from 08:00 to 20:00. The cafe is closed on Mondays for equipment checks and staff training.

The quiet room is called the Blue Orbit. Guests may reserve it for study groups of up to six people. Reservations last 90 minutes.

The cafe telescope is named Faraday. It is used for public skywatching every Saturday at 19:30, weather permitting. The workshop is free, but seats are limited to twelve guests.

The emergency supply locker is behind the north bookshelf. Its access code is not stored in this document. Staff must contact the duty manager instead.

The house rule is simple: ask before moving a telescope, keep drinks away from control panels, and leave every map where you found it.
